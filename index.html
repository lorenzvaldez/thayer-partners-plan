05 / Where I'd Start

Five things that can help your team now.

Each one stands on its own, helps right away, and becomes a building block of the future portal. Everything is built in Thayer's own accounts, so you own all of it.

1 to 2 weeksNo client data

The Thayer Brain

An internal assistant that knows your procedures, compliance manual, partner program, and FAQs. Anyone on the team asks a question and gets an answer from your own documents in seconds.

Why it helps: fewer interruptions across six offices, faster answers, and new hires and partner firms get up to speed without waiting on someone.

Built with: Claude on Thayer's business account

How it works
  1. Gather your procedures, compliance documents, and partner materials.
  2. Set it up in your Claude account and test it with real staff questions.
  3. Train the team in one session and refine based on feedback.
3 to 4 weeksBusiness info only

Partner Onboarding System

When a new firm joins your platform, one system sends the welcome, collects documents, tracks every step, sends reminders, and shows exactly who's stuck where.

Why it helps: every new partner firm onboarded the same polished way, with less manual follow-up and a better first impression for the firms you're recruiting.

Built with: HubSpot, workflow automation, Claude for drafting

How it works
  1. Map your current onboarding steps with your operations team.
  2. Automate the repetitive parts: forms, documents, emails, and reminders.
  3. Launch with a simple tracker showing each firm's progress.
2 to 3 weeksNo client data

Commentary to Content

Each piece of market commentary from your Chief Investment Officer becomes a blog post, a client email, and LinkedIn posts, written in Thayer's voice.

Why it helps: more visibility with clients, prospects, and partner firms, without adding work for your advisors.

Built with: Claude, HubSpot, a required compliance approval step

How it works
  1. Commentary is published or dropped into a shared folder.
  2. Drafts for each channel are created automatically in your voice.
  3. Compliance approves before anything goes out.
2 to 3 weeksUses a vetted vendor

Meeting Prep and Follow-Up

Before each client meeting, a one-page brief. After it, notes, a follow-up email draft, and tasks, ready for the advisor to review.

Why it helps: one of the biggest time savers in the industry for advisors serving hundreds of client relationships.

Built with: an established advisor AI tool, chosen and set up for your team

How it works
  1. Compare the leading advisor tools, including what you saw at Future Proof.
  2. Run the choice through your compliance review.
  3. Set it up, connect it to your CRM, and train the advisors.
2 to 4 weeksSample data only

Portal Prototype

A clickable version of your future portal, using sample data, so your team and partner firms can see it and react before any big investment.

Why it helps: decisions based on real feedback instead of guesses, and a clear spec for whoever builds the final version.

Built with: Claude Code, tracked in Thayer's own GitHub

06 / Security First

Client data protection is the starting point.

The SEC's 2024 Regulation S-P amendments raised the bar for protecting customer information and overseeing service providers. Every step of this plan is built around that.

No client data in early work

Assessment, prototypes, and quick wins run on documents and sample data only.

Business-grade AI only

Enterprise accounts where your data isn't used to train models, with admin controls.

Your accounts, your ownership

Everything lives in Thayer's accounts. My access can be removed anytime.

Compliance approves every vendor

Each tool comes with the security questions your compliance team needs answered.

09 / Built to Grow

Start lean, scale up with the right team.

Everything starts small and in your accounts. When the portal needs to handle real client data at full scale, a specialized team joins, and I lead that project for you so nothing gets lost.

Stage 1

Foundation

Assessment, quick wins, and the prototype. Fast, low risk, no client data.

Who: me, in Thayer's accounts

Stage 2

Connect

Chosen platforms set up and connected, workflows expanded across the team and partner firms.

Who: me, with your vendors

Stage 3

Full portal

Production portal with client data, custodian connections, and security testing.

Who: a specialized development team, with me as project lead

When it's time for Stage 3

  • The portal needs to store or display real client data like SSNs or account details
  • It needs a live connection to Schwab or your reporting system
  • Clients or partner firms log in, not just your internal team
  • It has to run for many users with ongoing security monitoring

Everything built in Stages 1 and 2 is documented, so a larger team can pick it up cleanly.
